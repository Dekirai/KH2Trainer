Actor/STATUS binding writers, 2026-10-08 — bounded static evidence

Decision: the five STATUS entry observers do not cover outer Actor/STATUS
rebinding. The new STATUS components should stay uninstalled until a separate
binding/lifetime admission proof exists.

Concrete findings
1. 4112B0 releases the old STATUS, obtains another through 3C06B0, writes
   Actor+5C0 at 4112D7 and encoded Actor into STATUS+268 at 4112EE.
   A 3C06B0 cache hit only increments refcount (+264) at 3C07CA and returns;
   it does not call Init/Rebuild. The earlier Release observer already ended.
2. 411350 similarly releases, allocates via 3C0280 and writes the binding at
   411377/41138E after observed initialization returns. Script thunks 431AA0
   and 431A70 reach these two routines. Their thread provenance remains open.
3. 3A7A40 publishes CurrentPlayer before storing Actor+5C0/STATUS+268.
   3C2DB0 has another paired construction store at 3C2E56/3C2E6D.
4. 3A80E0 parks CurrentPlayer in global 2A11268 and clears the current global.
   3A8140 restores it and clears the parking slot. OffCurrent is not retirement.
5. 3BB2D0 writes +5C0 on WINDOW_BASE. 3BC990/3BCA40 store WINDOW_BALLOON
   pointers at receiver+5C0; those receiver roles remain unclosed. Matching an
   offset is not enough to call a store an Actor/STATUS mutation.

Evidence and verification
19 complete function bodies, 1,281 instructions and 6,368 original PE bytes.
All full-body ASM/decompile pages are complete; verifier checks retail SHA,
exact bytes and instruction boundaries. Fresh verifier PASS.

88 contiguous search_text chunks traverse .text and .nep, returning 732 raw
text hits. Every returned cursor is done, but this is not a complete semantic
writer exclusion: the renderer omits some instruction lines behind labels or
comments. Four missing CurrentPlayer xrefs were recovered independently and
are loads. All 186 recorded direct CurrentPlayer xrefs decode from original PE;
their five direct stores are 3A7133/3A7AF4/3A8132/3A8163/3A8F43.

The operand-query known-answer probe also failed for real displaced-memory
stores. It is documented, not used as negative evidence. Twelve bytepattern
queries and 508 bounded instruction windows supplement the text scan. Arbitrary
window-start rows are discarded because insn_query can render a containing
instruction at a non-head address. All query cursors and raw results are saved.
