BDX BANK INVENTORY: OFFLINE CATALOG INTEGRATION
Version 0.12.13 — 2026-10-08

Result

The catalog now records 1,063 original descriptors across the eleven observed
bank slots. It contains 158 descriptive annotations. This adds 913 records and
54 annotations to v0.12.12 while preserving all 104 prior annotated records.
The native catalog API, decoder, viewmodel, XAML/view codebehind and 52 bridge
source files are byte-identical to the previous reviewed implementation.

These are captured structural extents. They are not a native array-length or
safe-index contract. Banks 3 and 9 retain ContextInstalled metadata because
their original registry slots are NULL. A recorded descriptor does not prove
the table is currently registered or its arguments and object lifetimes valid.
Most newly inventoried handlers remain unannotated.

What was transferred

The two structural inventories contribute 466 Bank1/2 records and 451 records
from the other newly inventoried banks. Four Bank1/2 records already existed,
so the net increase is 913. The builder rejects numeric disagreement for an
existing entry and does not overwrite an existing annotation.

The 54 new descriptions comprise:
* 41 Bank9 calls: model objects, motion/group helpers, camera fields, native
  transitions and deferred VM retirement. Source-specific limits remain visible.
* Four timer/message-related calls from the separately reviewed script/native
  study. Its source evidence does not make arbitrary supplied native effects safe.
* Two Actor vector/query calls, including shared result storage, validation
  after Actor reads and conditional float32 non-progress at 2^31. No retail
  hang or universal geometric meaning is asserted.
* Six actual RET-only handlers and the conditional Actor target-link writer.

Six RET-only entries remain nonnull targets. Their VM descriptors still consume
their declared operands and declare no return slot. Fifteen NULL descriptor
holes remain distinct. Three Bank4 targets are real branch stubs even though
the original IDB did not define them as functions. Aliased handler addresses
retain their separate bank/index identities.

For 2:97, the immediate path stores the encoded second pointer at first-object
offset +3384. The store at +3392 is conditional; a failed predicate leaves that
field unchanged. The annotation does not turn list membership into a stable
lifetime reservation or claim a STATUS mutation.

The 9:27 text includes the independent review correction: a NULL target skips
existing per-object attachment stores, and a NULL effective camera skips the
object camera store. Supplying NULL does not mean these fields are cleared.

All 104 prior annotated rows, including the Actor+4 self-link clarification for
2:9/95, remain exactly unchanged. No new Event27 rebind witness is inferred.
