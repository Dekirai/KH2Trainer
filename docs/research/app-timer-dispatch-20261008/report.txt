Practical implications
A future read-only event viewer could label timer expiry as Actor BDX event10 with two typed integers, distinguish missing handlers, and expose the concrete class descriptor and Script binding.
Safe reversible Actor/STATUS edits still require a proven stable lifetime boundary. Actor-list membership, the VM owner count and two cleanup passes do not independently supply that boundary.
The next semantic work is selected event10/message74 script paths and the two sibling registries. Actor script selection and native-trap effects must stay explicit; unknown traps cannot be silently treated as no-ops.
