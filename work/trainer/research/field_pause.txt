SEMANTICS
This pauses the complete field scheduler, including its actor/controller/BDX
fibers, simulation tasks and field rendering. The outer scheduler still draws
the captured game image and presents frames. Mission clock updates are masked.
Audio, outer UI and general/per-world SaveData play-time counters continue.
This is distinct from slot79, which freezes only actors and selected effects.
The frozen world cannot be viewed from a moving free camera. Step runs one
complete field dispatch plus its timing update, then captures and pauses again.
It is not a fixed-duration, deterministic emulation-frame promise; native delta,
controller input and integer fiber waits retain their existing semantics.
