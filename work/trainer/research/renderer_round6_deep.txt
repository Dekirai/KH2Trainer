Renderer round6: actual resolution and AA controls
==================================================
Windowed/maximized native requests are ready for guarded implementation. Actual fixed/capped MSAA has an identified normal-frame decision boundary preserving replay and resource creation, but needs a separately verified callsite-hook implementation. Fullscreen enumeration has an empty-set recursion edge.

Capture: 63 full bodies / 9640 instructions / 42411 original function bytes.
Semantic claims: 52; remaining captured functions are raw support, not new semantic coverage.
