Additional capture ownership review:156720,154EE0 and394690 acquire native
capture1 without directly changing field state. They are scheduled as inner
field tasks at priority300000. A practice Step can run these tasks and thereby
give capture ownership to a native transition. Therefore the existing cleanup
must NOT be widened to clear capture1 whenever field state is1; doing so can
erase that new native capture. The conservative state2/same-scene/non-takeover
condition is preferable. If an independent mod merely changes state2 to1 while
leaving capture1, that same-bit ownership cannot be fully distinguished.
