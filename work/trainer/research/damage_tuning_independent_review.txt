Damage coefficient contract - independent static review
=======================================================
Reviewer: native_debug. No source/game/IDB changes or live test.
Full fresh dedicated ASM: damage_tuning_independent_evidence.json,
10 functions / 1262 actual instruction lines, paginated in blocks of100;
every collected instruction count equals total_instructions.

Confirmed:
- STATUS+424..429 are six unsigned byte element coefficients; +430 is
  the general received-hit coefficient. Constructors3C0010 and3C1D90
  initialize all seven to100; equipment3C1190 multiplies them by an
  item coefficient divided by100 with upward integer rounding.
- Enemy reconstruction3C2240 reads low bytes from seven16-bit fields
  EnemyParam+72..84. Native3C2650 scales the selected coefficient against
  its original16-bit enemy parameter, then stores a byte. VM431200 calls
  it with three8-byte operands. It is not a universally suitable setter
  for Player because STATUS+624 is an EnemyParam reference.
- 3C12F0 multiplies normal damage by general*element coefficients in
  signed32-bit intermediate arithmetic. The target's STATUS is passed
  by3CE740/3CEE90; these are received-damage coefficients, not outgoing
  power multipliers. The byte representation permits0..255.
- Coefficient product0 additionally sets Hit+24 bit4 and clears Hit+38
  in both3CE740 and3CEE90. Thus0 is more than a cosmetic numeric zero.
- Normal save synchronization3C2120 copies HP/MP/timers/Drive fields,
  not424..430. A one-time edit is runtime state and may be overwritten
  by equipment, scripts, status rebuild or actor replacement.

Contract caveats:
- Attack type5/6 takes an earlier3C12F0 arithmetic branch that bypasses
  the coefficient multiplication. Do not promise an absolute universal
  damage multiplier or complete invulnerability. Other later modifiers
  (difficulty, limits, elemental boosts, Anti and HP thresholds) remain.
- Preserve a freshly validated current Player or manually locked living
  Enemy identity/status/backlink and pool ownership. Do not reuse across
  Actor/form/scene changes. Byte writes should be one-shot, no hidden
  recurring enforcement or changes to the original EnemyParam table.
- A raw0..255 byte cannot itself overflow storage, but extreme modified
  attack data may overflow the native signed32-bit damage intermediates.
  This review does not establish arbitrary modded asset arithmetic.
- No new blocker to the proposed one-shot runtime contract found.
