Final behavior and resolved findings
-----------------------------------
The override writes once on enable or a requested rate change. It survives
ordinary motion changes on the same current Sora/model and ends on native
script ownership, actor/form/scene identity loss, lost callback/table ownership,
or observed foreign value. It does not continuously overwrite the field.

The callback observes native ownership before invoking immutable Original,
including identical-float writes and an expired host. Original is forwarded
exactly once. An exclusive SRW lock serializes this observation plus Original
with baseline capture, explicit writes and restoration. A finally path releases
the lock when native exceptions propagate.

Restoration now rechecks ScenePhase and the freshly resolved/listed actor,
status/backlink, owner, form, heap, schedulers and room. An explicitly unready
context never restores. TableReady and OwnSlot are checked inside Stop's lock,
so direct Reset and HostExpired cannot bypass no-restore behavior after slot or
bank loss. Start and rate updates also recheck relevant gates under the lock.

Model, model-resource and resource-vtable identity bind the lease. A model or
motion validity loss may compare-restore only on the same currently valid
actor: the speed field belongs to Actor, independently of the motion resource.
The wrapper is resident and hook removal uses own-to-native CAS, preserving
foreign slot changes and already-dispatched callback forwarding.

Tests inspected include concurrent same-value writer versus Reset, direct
Reset and host expiry before Tick, lost slot/bank on those same paths, exception
forwarding with lock release, wrong-thread guards, actor/scene changes, model and
resource-vtable changes, protection/CAS failures and validated readouts.
Detailed findings and their resolutions are in motion_independent_review.txt;
structured verdict, test attribution and artifact hashes are in the paired JSON.

Practical limits
----------------
No live behavior is established by this static review. Scoped direct-writer
searches cannot exclude computed aliases, whole-object copies or external mods.
Our lock cannot synchronize arbitrary writers that bypass the wrapper.
Identical raw external writes and identity ABA changes between observations may
be undetectable. The exact inspected native callback has no recursive callback
path; unrelated runtime code changes are outside this contract.

Motion speed also affects root motion and trigger timing. The 0.1..3 range is a
trainer policy, not an engine-proven safety range. Readouts avoid evaluation or
pose mutators; no arbitrary seek, motion-ID write or zero-speed freeze is added.

Only research artifacts were written by this reviewer. No product, Native,
Common, game, UI or IDB mutations were performed.
