Scope and evidence
Read the complete module and relevant tests, plus the shared actor_movement::Ready and damage_tuning::PoolOwns/Ready dependencies. Compared the selective bit write with the saved native predicates and body resolver in actor_collision_round4_evidence.json. Complete recorded counts match:3BA940=14 instructions,3BA970=17,3B9090=575. The two predicates and the resolver's two-sided eligibility checks confirm the intended bit meaning. Existing decompiler exports of40D000 and its transfer helpers were also inspected for an obvious bulk transfer of the old body-separation flag; no such copy appeared in those listed transfer bodies. This is not an independent exhaustive re-audit of every possible actor flag writer.

Confirmed behavior
- Only Actor+288 mask2 changes.3BA970 excludes that actor from body separation, and3B9090 checks this predicate for both participants.
- World collision uses a distinct mask/gate; hit shapes and HP/status are not written by the trainer module.
- Enable requires current Sora identity, valid active status-pool ownership, stable native scene and a valid nonempty type1 body buffer. Native preexisting mask2 is not acquired.
- The lease binds actor/status/form/map/scheduler/heap. Restore reevaluates allocation/list/backlink/scene identity and never tries to reinsert an old actor.
- Masked compare-exchange preserves unrelated flag bits. Observed removal of mask2 ends ownership without reapplying it. A compare-exchange conflict deliberately ends the lease without forcing a value.
- Normal field pause can retain the lease; explicit reset/heartbeat expiry can release it while actor identity is still valid. Pending scene/Drive transition, actor replacement or deletion abandons the old pointer without a write.
- Diagnostic reads avoid native probe getters and retain no collider pointer. Contact interpretation is suppressed for known stopped/paused/bypassed states and labeled as cached, with no claim of a per-frame generation stamp.
